## Project Summary
AI-assisted voice booking workflow designed for Right Outside Auto Detailing LLC to support customer service conversations, pricing confirmation, and appointment booking through structured stages, prompt engineering, and iterative testing.

## Business Problem
Missed calls and unstructured booking conversations created process risk: incomplete customer data, unclear service requests, pricing confusion, and appointments that could not be confirmed confidently.

## My Responsibilities
- Designed complete customer conversation flow
- Wrote and refined conversation logic and prompts
- Developed business rules and pricing logic
- Designed booking workflow stages
- Tested repeatedly and documented failures/fixes
- Improved customer experience through structured workflow design

## Workflow Design
Stages designed:
1. Greeting / call intake
2. Service qualification
3. Vehicle identification
4. Pricing
5. Pricing confirmation
6. Customer name collection
7. Service address + ZIP collection
8. Appointment options
9. Appointment confirmation
10. Professional close

## Prompt Engineering
- One-question-at-a-time instructions
- Stage-transition controls
- Confirmation checkpoints for pricing and appointments
- Iterative refinement after observed failures

## Testing Methodology
- Walk through realistic booking scenarios
- Compare expected vs. actual assistant behavior
- Cover edge cases: incomplete answers, address issues, pricing confirmation, order errors
- Retest after each prompt/logic change

## Root Cause Analysis
Classify each failure as:
- Prompt ambiguity
- Missing business rule
- Weak stage boundary
- Incorrect sequencing
- Incomplete confirmation logic

## Quality Assurance Process
1. Define expected stage behavior  
2. Run scenario test  
3. Record observed behavior  
4. Classify failure  
5. Apply targeted fix  
6. Retest  
7. Document issue, cause, fix, remaining risk  

## Lessons Learned
- Stage design and checkpoints improve reliability as much as wording changes
- Confirmation steps are critical quality controls
- Documentation turns testing into reusable AI Operations knowledge
