Projects / Voice

AI Voice Booking Assistant

An AI-assisted voice booking workflow designed for Right Outside Auto Detailing LLC to guide callers through qualification, pricing confirmation, information collection, and appointment confirmation.

Functional Build Docs + diagrams published

Business problem

Missed or unstructured booking conversations create incomplete details, pricing confusion, and appointments that cannot be confirmed confidently. Process risk includes incomplete customer data, unclear service requests, and unreliable confirmation.

Workflow design

Built a structured 10-stage booking flow with one-question-at-a-time behavior, stage control, and confirmation checkpoints. Exact proprietary prompt text is withheld from the public portfolio.

Design-level stages — not a production call transcript or telephony export.

Ten stages

  1. Greeting
  2. Service qualification
  3. Vehicle identification
  4. Pricing
  5. Pricing confirmation
  6. Customer name
  7. Address / ZIP
  8. Appointment options
  9. Confirmation
  10. Professional close

Prompt engineering

Enforced one-question-at-a-time behavior, stage control, and confirmation checkpoints; refined prompts after observed failures such as loops, repeated questions, incorrect booking order, address handling issues, and pricing confirmation gaps.

Testing & QA

Applied a structured methodology: define expected behavior → test → classify failures → root cause analysis → fix → retest → document. Iterative testing notes show fewer recurring failure patterns — qualitative only; no production call metrics claimed.
