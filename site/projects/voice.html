Business problem

Missed or unstructured booking conversations create incomplete details, pricing confusion, and appointments that cannot be confirmed confidently.

Workflow design

Built a structured 10-stage booking flow with one-question-at-a-time behavior, stage control, and confirmation checkpoints. Proprietary prompt text is withheld.

Design-level stages — not a production call transcript.

Ten stages

  1. Greeting
  2. Service qualification
  3. Vehicle identification
  4. Pricing
  5. Pricing confirmation
  6. Customer name
  7. Address / ZIP
  8. Appointment options
  9. Confirmation
  10. Professional close

Prompt engineering & QA

Refined prompts after observed failures: loops, repeated questions, incorrect booking order, address handling issues, and pricing confirmation gaps. Method: define expected behavior → test → classify → root cause → fix → retest → document.
