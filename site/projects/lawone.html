Problem

Legal research materials are fragmented across jurisdictions and document types; users need organized search with clear source labeling.

Solution

Built a Next.js App Router platform foundation with federal/state/maritime research UI, search filters, change alerts UI, assistant scaffolding, and a national catalog framework — with demonstration records labeled in-product.

My role

Product/architecture documentation and platform UI development across documented phases A–H.

Tools used

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vitest, Supabase schema foundations, official connector stubs where available.

Challenges

Keeping legal-information disclaimers clear; not inventing citations; separating catalog completeness from ingested law.

What I improved

Clearer research answer format policies, source-health thinking, and labeled demo experiences for evaluation.

Verified outcome

Phases A–H documented complete with explicit constraints: no live LLM, no live Auth/Stripe in public claims; catalog ≠ ingested law. UI screenshots captured from the local app.
