## Purpose
Airtable supports AI-assisted workflow organization by planning records, status fields, and process-tracking structures for booking operations concepts and GH-X digital product workflow concepts.

## Business Problem
Without structured records and status tracking, AI-assisted and manual workflow steps become harder to review, hand off, and improve. Data needs to mirror workflow stages so process progress is visible and testable.

## My Responsibilities
- Designed Airtable table and field structures aligned to workflow stages
- Planned status tracking and confirmation-checkpoint fields
- Designed a separate issue-logging structure for QA and troubleshooting
- Planned automation touchpoints between Airtable and n8n workflow concepts
- Structured fields to serve as clear inputs for AI-assisted steps
- Documented structure decisions to support review and iteration
