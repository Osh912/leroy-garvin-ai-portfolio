# GH-X — Business Problem

## Problem Statement
Digital product and business workflows often depend on repetitive manual steps: organizing information, moving tasks through stages, applying consistent AI instructions, and tracking process status. Without structured automation logic, prompt strategy, and data planning, these workflows become inconsistent and harder to review.

## Who Is Affected
- The operator managing digital product workflow steps
- Future reviewers who need to understand process status and quality
- Any AI-assisted step that depends on clear inputs, outputs, and handoffs

## Current State (Before)
Workflow ideas existed in an ad hoc form without a layered architecture for process, prompts, automation, data, and quality control. Process steps, AI instructions, and status tracking were not clearly separated, which made testing and documentation harder.

## Desired State (After)
A testable AI Operations / Workflow Automation concept with:
- Defined workflow stages
- Prompt strategies aligned to each stage
- n8n workflow ideas for sequencing and handoffs
- Airtable planning for records and status tracking
- Testing, root cause analysis, and process documentation for continuous improvement

## Constraints
- Work began as concept design and iterative testing, not a verified enterprise deployment
- Documentation and clarity were required before complexity increased
- Prompt and automation steps had to remain reviewable
- No invented user volume, revenue, or production-scale claims

## Success Criteria (Qualitative Only)
- Clear workflow architecture and stage definitions
- Documented prompt strategies and testing notes
- Identified weak points and refinement paths
- Reusable process documentation for future automation work
- Clearer separation between prompt issues and sequencing issues during troubleshooting

These criteria are process-quality measures. No deployment metrics, user counts, or revenue outcomes are claimed.

## Why This Matters for Recruiters
GH-X demonstrates AI Operations and Workflow Automation thinking applied to digital product operations through structured design, prompt engineering, testing, and documentation—skills used in AI Support, Prompt Engineering Support, AI Quality Assurance, and related roles.
