# GH-X — My Role

## Role Title / Capacity
Independent AI Automation & Workflow Designer  
*(AI Operations · Workflow Automation · Prompt Engineering · Process Documentation)*

## Responsibilities
- Designed AI-powered business automation concepts for digital product operations
- Built workflow ideas in n8n for stage sequencing and handoffs
- Created and refined prompt strategies for AI-assisted workflow stages
- Planned Airtable database structures for records and status tracking
- Designed workflow logic, decision points, and review checkpoints
- Tested automation ideas and evaluated AI-assisted outputs
- Performed root cause analysis to separate prompt issues from sequencing and documentation gaps
- Documented processes, issues, fixes, and continuous improvement notes
- Applied iterative development and AI quality assurance practices

## Decisions Made
- Used a layered architecture: process, prompt, automation, data, quality
- Treated prompt engineering as workflow control, not isolated text writing
- Prioritized documentation and testability over premature complexity
- Separated prompt failures from sequencing failures during troubleshooting
- Kept claims at concept/design/test level rather than unsupported production claims
