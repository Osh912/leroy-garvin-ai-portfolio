# n8n Workflow — Project Summary

## One-Line Summary
Designed and tested n8n Workflow Automation concepts for stage sequencing, prompt-assisted review handoffs, and failure logging to support AI Operations workflows.

## Business Problem
Manual repetitive steps and unclear handoffs reduce operational consistency and make AI-assisted processes harder to test and improve.

## What Was Designed / Tested
- Booking process sequencing concept
- GH-X digital product stage handoff concept
- Prompt-assisted step review flow
- Reusable templates for sequencing, review, and failure logging

## Outcomes (Qualitative Only)
- Weak transitions easier to identify
- Concepts clearer and more testable
- Prompt issues separable from sequencing issues during review

## Technologies
n8n · Airtable (related concepts) · ChatGPT / Claude (prompt-assisted steps) · Process documentation

## Best Role Fit
Workflow Automation Specialist · AI Operations · AI Support Engineer · Prompt Engineering · AI Quality Assurance
