# AI Voice Booking Assistant — Project Summary

## One-Line Summary
Independently designed, tested, and documented an AI-assisted voice booking workflow for Right Outside Auto Detailing LLC using prompt engineering, structured testing, and process documentation.

## Business Problem
Missed calls and unstructured booking conversations created risk of incomplete customer data, unclear service requests, pricing confusion, and unreliable appointment confirmation.

## What Was Built / Documented
- 10-stage booking conversation flow
- Conversation logic, business rules, and pricing logic
- Prompt strategies with confirmation checkpoints
- Scenario testing, root cause analysis, and iterative refinements
- Supporting concepts with n8n, Airtable, and Twilio at documented scope

## Outcomes (Qualitative Only)
- Clearer stage control and confirmation checkpoints
- Reduced common failure patterns (loops, repeated questions, incorrect order, pricing confirmation gaps, address handling issues) through iterative testing
- Reusable QA and documentation process

## Technologies
ChatGPT · Prompt engineering · Workflow design · n8n (concepts) · Airtable (concepts) · Twilio (voice technology set)

## Best Role Fit
Conversational AI Testing · Prompt Engineering · AI Quality Assurance · AI Operations · AI Trainer · AI Support Engineer
